# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 4, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 4, 2026

Mobile Craft Bars LLC ("Mobile Craft Bars," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you visit our website, request a quote, book a discovery call, or use our bartending services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site or services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide to us.** When you fill out our discovery form, book a call, or hire us for an event, you may provide:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number (if you opt in to text messaging)
- Mailing address (for invoicing or contracts where applicable)
- Event details (event type, date, guest count, venue, budget, preferences, dietary or allergen notes)
- Payment information (processed by our third-party payment processor; we do not store full card numbers)
- Any other information you choose to share with us

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

- IP address and approximate location (city, region, country)
- Browser type and version, operating system, device type
- Pages you visit on our site, time spent, links clicked, scroll depth
- Referring website and ad click identifiers (such as `fbclid` from Meta or `utm_source` from any platform)
- Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 6)

We do not process sensitive personal information (such as health data, biometrics, or precise geolocation), and we do not collect personal information from third parties.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Respond to your inquiry and prepare a custom quote
- Schedule, confirm, and conduct your event
- Send appointment confirmations and reminders by email and (if you opt in) SMS
- Process payments and keep records of transactions
- Improve our website, service offerings, and ad targeting
- Measure the performance of our marketing campaigns
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

We process your information only when we have a valid legal basis to do so, such as your consent, the performance of a contract, our legitimate business interests, or legal compliance.

---

## 3. Text Messaging (SMS)

If you provide your phone number and consent at the point of collection, we may send you appointment-related text messages.

**What you'll receive.** Booking confirmations, reminders before your event, day-of logistics, and follow-up messages directly related to your event with us. We do not send marketing or promotional texts unless you separately opt in to receive them.

**Frequency.** Up to four (4) messages per booking. Frequency depends on the event timeline and any rescheduling.

**Costs.** Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan. Mobile Craft Bars does not charge for the messages themselves.

**How to opt out.** Reply **STOP** or **UNSUBSCRIBE** to any text message to stop receiving messages. You may also reply **HELP** for assistance or contact us at the email below. Opting out of SMS will not affect your ability to receive email confirmations or our service in general.

**Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.**

We use Twilio, Inc. as our SMS messaging service provider. By providing your phone number and consenting to receive texts, you also acknowledge that your phone number will be processed by Twilio for the purpose of sending these messages. See https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy for Twilio's privacy practices.

**Mobile information sharing.** No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with third parties.

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, your event is being planned or executed, or as needed to provide services and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. After that period, we delete or anonymize the information.

Specific retention periods:

- Lead and discovery-call inquiries: up to 24 months
- Booked event records and contracts: 7 years (for tax and legal compliance)
- Marketing analytics data: up to 36 months in aggregated form

When we no longer have a legitimate need to retain your information, we delete or anonymize it. If deletion is not immediately possible (for example, because the information has been stored in backup archives), we will isolate it from further processing until deletion is possible.

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including:

- **Analytics cookies** (PostHog, Google Analytics) that help us understand how the site is used
- **Advertising cookies** (Meta Pixel) that help us measure the effectiveness of ads and show you relevant ads on other sites you visit

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may impact how the site functions for you.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where required by law.

## 7. Do Not Track Signals

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this time, no uniform standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other automatic mechanism. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we are required to follow, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Policy.
